# Validation Checklist

Run this checklist on each draft after applying the master language rule set. Items are drawn from all 5 v2.4 synthesis runs.

---

## Critical — Fix before publishing

**Prohibited patterns**
- [ ] Does the first sentence of each section name the actual subject and do something with it? (DC1, P4)
- [ ] Are *delve, navigate* (metaphorical), *harness, unlock, embark, unveil, unpack* absent from the draft? (P1)
- [ ] Are *tapestry, realm, mosaic, beacon* (metaphorical), *double-edged sword, at the intersection of* absent? (P2)
- [ ] Is the opening free of grand-opening clichés (*In today's fast-paced world, In the ever-evolving landscape of, As technology continues to evolve*)? (P3)
- [ ] Are meta-announcement sentences absent (*This article will explore, In this guide we will, Let's dive into*)? (P4)
- [ ] Are the specific padding phrases absent: *It's worth noting that, It is important to note that, It is worth mentioning that*? (P5)
- [ ] Is the draft free of generic benefit-wrap closers (*By doing so you can, This approach allows you to, This enables you to focus on what matters most*)? (P6)
- [ ] Are quantitative inflation metaphors absent (*treasure trove, plethora, a wealth of, game-changer, paradigm shift*)? (P7)
- [ ] Does the draft avoid attributing human thought or emotion to code, systems, or AI (*the code thinks, the model believes, the framework wants*)? (P8)

**Recommendations and stance**
- [ ] When the evidence supports a recommendation, is it stated directly — not hidden behind both-sides framing? (DC3)
- [ ] Does the draft avoid implying that any marker is proof of AI authorship? These are editorial signals, not attribution tools.

**Voice and accuracy**
- [ ] Has the writer's voice survived the edit — idiom, humor, opinion, and idiosyncratic word choices intact? (Section 7)
- [ ] Have all factual claims, numbers, and dates been verified against a reliable external source before publishing?

---

## Important — Fix before sharing externally

**Limited patterns within threshold**
- [ ] Are adjective inflation instances (*robust, seamless, pivotal, crucial, vital* as evaluative terms) at ≤1 per 500 words each? (L1)
- [ ] Is *In conclusion / In summary / To summarize* used at most once per document, with strong preference for zero? (L2)
- [ ] Are paragraph-starting formal transitions (*Furthermore, Moreover, Additionally, Consequently*) at ≤2 per 500 words, and never in consecutive paragraphs? (L3)
- [ ] Are vague benefit verbs (*enhance, streamline, optimize, foster, facilitate*) paired with a mechanism or replaced? (L4)
- [ ] Are caveat prefaces (*That said, It's important to, You should*) at ≤1 per 300 words? (L5)
- [ ] Did you replace broad importance adjectives with named consequences wherever possible? (L1)
- [ ] Has the *not only...but also* construction been reduced to ≤1 per 800 words? (L7)

**Cadence and structure**
- [ ] Does sentence length vary? Per 500 words: at least 1 sentence under 8 words and 1 over 30 words. (SC1)
- [ ] Is there a run of 4+ consecutive sentences within 5 words of the same length? Fix if yes. (L8)
- [ ] Do fewer than 3 consecutive paragraphs share the same shape (broad claim → list → takeaway)? (L9)
- [ ] Are triple-adjective stacks (*powerful, flexible, and intuitive*) at ≤1 per 500 words? (L10)
- [ ] Are formal triads (*A, B, and C*) at ≤1 per 500 words? (SC2)
- [ ] Is the tricolon-plus-participial construction (*fast, cheap, and reliable, ensuring...*) absent from consecutive paragraphs? (SC3)

**Discourse and punctuation**
- [ ] Does each section end on a concrete point, decision, or implication — not a restatement? (DC2)
- [ ] Are em dashes at ≤1 per 300 words, with no double em-dash pairs in one paragraph? (PC1)
- [ ] Do no more than 2 paragraphs in any section start with the same grammatical structure? (SC4)
- [ ] Is bilateral hedging (*While X, Y also has merit; both options have merit*) at ≤1 per 500 words? (L6)

---

## Optional — Review for quality

- [ ] Does each formal transition (*Furthermore, Moreover*) do real connective work, or can it be cut?
- [ ] Are punctuation patterns varied? If one pattern (dash, colon, semicolon) dominates more than 40% of paragraph endings, reduce it. (PC4)
- [ ] Is the draft free of colon-then-capitalized inline pseudo-lists (*There are three things: First... Second...*) unless a real list is appropriate? (L13)
- [ ] Does the text use contractions where a human expert speaking to a peer would? (L14)
- [ ] Are *at its core, fundamentally, essentially* essence-claims limited to 1 per document and immediately justified? (L11)
- [ ] In creative prose: was intentional rhythm — including deliberate rule breaks — preserved even where it conflicts with a general rule? (Section 7)
- [ ] Has at least one banned-pattern correction been interrogated to check whether reverting it would improve the sentence?
